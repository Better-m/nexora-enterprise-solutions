Before publishing:
1. Replace hello@yourdomain.co.za in script.js with the official company email address.
2. Add the official phone/WhatsApp number and published domain.
3. Confirm CIPC registration and company name approval before presenting the business as an incorporated Pty Ltd.
4. Add a privacy notice and configure a secure form handler if you want enquiries submitted without opening the visitor's email app.
5. Check all service claims and ensure any regulated services are only offered after applicable requirements are met.

This is a static responsive website. It does not include a server, database, payment gateway, CRM or working server-side enquiry form.
